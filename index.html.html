Rules for choosing the perfect rug for your room

Whether you live alone or with family, the living room works hardest. Keep these five points in mind when you shop.

  • Check that the rug is the right size for the space.
  • Pick a colour and pattern that complement the existing decor.
  • Match the style of the rug to the room, traditional or modern.
  • Let the shape of the room guide the shape of the rug.
  • Consider the material, and how much traffic the room gets.

Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between a rug and a carpet?

A carpet usually covers a whole room wall to wall. A rug is a finished piece that covers one area, such as under a coffee table or bed.

Do you deliver across Pakistan?

Yes. We deliver from Rawalpindi with careful packing. Message us on WhatsApp with your city for the delivery charge.

How do I choose the right rug size?

Leave at least the front legs of your sofa and chairs on the rug. This ties the seating area together.

Do you supply masjid carpets?

Yes. We supply saf carpets and jaynamaz rolls for masjids and can cut them to the hall dimensions.

How should I clean my Persian or Turkish rug?

Vacuum without a rotating brush, blot spills with a dry cloth instead of scrubbing, and use a professional rug wash for deep cleaning.
